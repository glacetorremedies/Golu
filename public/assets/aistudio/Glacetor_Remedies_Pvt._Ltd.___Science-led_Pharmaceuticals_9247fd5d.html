About Us

Trusted pharmaceutical brands, built on quality

Backed by 10+ years of hands-on pharmaceutical industry experience, Glacetor Remedies Pvt. Ltd. delivers a clinically focused portfolio of branded formulations across cardiology, diabetology, neuro-psychiatry, gastroenterology and clinical nutrition.

Every batch is manufactured and quality-tested at WHO-GMP and GLP certified facilities with strict stability and regulatory protocols—ensuring consistent therapeutic efficacy for doctors, chemists and patients.

Our mission: To make rational, quality-assured medicines reliably available to every doctor, healthcare partner and patient we serve.
